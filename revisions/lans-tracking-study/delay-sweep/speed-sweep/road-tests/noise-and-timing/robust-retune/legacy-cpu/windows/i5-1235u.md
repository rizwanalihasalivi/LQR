# Intel Core i5-1235U Windows timing

The submitted Windows x64 run used logical CPU 0 on an Intel Core i5-1235U. The portable benchmark timed each controller on 18,836 shared states over three repeats, for 56,508 decisions per controller. The maximum steering differences match the Xeon portable run.

| CSV rank | Controller | Median, μs | P95, μs | Mean, μs |
| ---: | --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| 1 | LQR | 0 | 0.1 | 0.0301 |
| 2 | LANS | 1.5 | 1.9 | 1.6894 |
| 3 | MPC | 1.5 | 860.865 | 183.2897 |
| 4 | PPO | 11.8 | 13.5 | 12.8708 |

The CSV assigns LANS rank 2 and MPC rank 3. Their reported medians tie at 1.5 μs. LANS has the lower P95 (1.9 μs versus 860.865 μs) and lower mean (1.6894 μs versus 183.2897 μs). LQR’s reported median of zero reflects the Windows timer resolution at this scale. Its measured mean is 0.0301 μs.

LANS’s median was 16.9% lower than the Xeon portable run’s 1.804 μs. The portable runs share the workload and controller implementations. They use different operating systems, build toolchains and clocks, so the measurements represent the complete systems.
